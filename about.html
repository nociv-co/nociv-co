NOCIV — Nothing Of Civilization, Explained

NOCIV stands for Nothing of Civilization.

NOCIV isn't about going backward.

Civilization wasn't necessarily a mistake, and the answer isn't to return to some previous version of the world. The problem is that civilization can become so large and complicated that people begin to see the world as something already finished.

The systems around us can feel permanent.
The things we use can feel disconnected from the people who made them.
We become consumers of a world we forget was created by people.

But it isn't finished.
We built this.
Which means we can build something else.

Civilization, as a word, also tells a false story: that we are separate from nature, when in truth there is no separation.

The outdoors was never some far away place.
We are always in it.

Turning clay into a brick, and that brick into a house, doesn't change where it came from. It's still earth.

The NOCIV mindset is about recognizing the connection between things that civilization teaches us to separate. That could mean:

  • Humans and nature
  • Creator and consumer
  • Product and owner
  • Art and everyday life
  • Technology and the people who use it
  • Thought and action

The goal isn't to return to some earlier version of the world.
It's to recognize that the world around us was made.
And because it was made, it can be changed.

The Creator's Dilemma

The Creator's Dilemma lives in the gap between vision and action.

Once you see what could be, you face a choice:

Ignore it.
Wait for someone else.
Or begin.

"We should own what we buy, even if that means creating it."

Creation reclaims narratives, sets new norms, and brings back genuine in-person connection. We share what we make so other people can see that change is possible.

The Creator's Dilemma only remains a dilemma if nothing is done.

You are a creator. We are creators. Let's put action behind what matters.
