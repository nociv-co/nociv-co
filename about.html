Nothing Of Civilization

It doesn't mean going backward. It means remembering that nothing around us is permanent. Every rule, every tool, every product was built by people.

We built this world. So we can build something else.

Every idea starts with a gap between vision and action. You can ignore it, wait for someone else, or begin.

We Are All Creators

"Maker" got claimed by tech. "Creator" got claimed by content. "Artist" means painter. "Polymath" sounds like a math test.

NOCIV uses the oldest meaning. A creator makes things.

So do you.

The site is where it starts, not where it ends. A shirt, a zine or a tool here should lead somewhere real: a pop-up, a conversation, or something you made with your own hands.

Arizona based. Not Arizona limited.

Born in California. Raised in Texas. Home in Arizona. A brand has to live somewhere to build real community, and this is where we host, craft and build in person.

The connections start here. They travel everywhere.

Representing Black America

NOCIV is Black American owned, and that shapes everything we make. Black Americans didn't just survive here. We built, adapted, and made a culture that keeps shaping the world.

We know who we are. We are not lost. We are here.

That means Black life in full: the regions, the styles, the contradictions. Not a caricature. Not a poster. Something living, active and present.

I make things.

I'm Jasilyn Anderson, also known as Lofty Lyn. Black American. A creative individual.

Who Is Behind NOCIV

My journey has been a lot of unlearning and learning. I start most things knowing nothing and figure them out one piece at a time. Art, textiles, screen printing, hardware, code, writing.

The words for people like me never fit. So I stopped looking for one.

That's why NOCIV sells clothes and gives the tools away.

Not "own nothing and be happy." Own what you make.

Write to me.

An order, a custom piece, a collab, a spot for a pop-up. It comes straight to me.
