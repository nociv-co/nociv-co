Edit your video

Pick a regular video from your camera roll, up to 5 minutes. Cut it, change the speed, add words, then use the deck buttons under the picture. Hit REC to record your edit.

Record and save

Your way: press the red REC button on the deck, then use PLAY, REW, FF and PAUSE like you are working the tape. Press REC again to finish. What you see is what you get.

Automatic: plays it for you and saves.
