Shoot

Prop your phone up so it cannot move. Move your subject a tiny bit, tap the red button, repeat. The faded picture on top is your last shot so you can see how far you moved.

The shape is locked once you have a frame. Clear all frames to change it.

Edit

Tap a frame in the strip under the picture to pick it. Move it, copy it, delete it. Copy a frame to make that moment last longer.

Look

Tap an era. You see it on the picture above, and it goes on everything you save.

Save

The video is recorded in real time, so a 10 second loop takes about 10 seconds. Keep this page open and your screen on while it records.

VHS a video

Pick a regular video from your camera roll, up to 5 minutes. It plays through the look you chose in step 3 and records a new copy. It runs in real time, so a 5 minute video takes 5 minutes. Keep this page open and your screen on.
