THE LAND INITIATIVE

Off-grid, non-profit, built in phases — starting now, with no land owned yet.

Residency Pods

1-month residency: a private aircrete dome studio with bed, storage, and A/C per pod.

Shared Facilities

Indoor/outdoor communal kitchen, solar showers, and composting setups.

Build Bays

Covered outdoor shop space for timber work, metal fabrication, and van or tiny home builds.

Hands-On Workshops

Community workshops teach aircrete dome construction, off-grid solar, rainwater harvesting, and natural building — by building the site in real time.
