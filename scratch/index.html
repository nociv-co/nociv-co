Good to know

  • Free kit, no sign up

The kit is free and always will be. The one thing you need is a domain name (like yourname.com). You buy it from a domain seller, not from us. A .com usually costs about $11 a year.

What you can change

Every base can turn into any other. Name your tabs anything and add as many as you need. Each tab is a kind of page: Home, Items, Page, Gallery, Links or Contact.

Choose the menu: tabs on top, a menu button, an app bottom bar or a side list. Choose how pages change: fade, slide, rise or zoom. Pick type, colors, corners, spacing, layout and photo shape. The studio checks that your text is readable.

Two accounts. That is it.

Cloudflare (free)Buys your domain, hosts your site, handles the security lock. One place.
Stripe (free to open)Pays you. Runs checkout, takes the shipping address, emails the receipt. Only needed if you sell.

Why not zero? Money has to land in an account with your name on it. That part cannot be skipped by anyone.

Put it online, step by step

  1. Build itTap what you are making, above. Fill in your pages. On Publish, tap Download my site. On iPhone it lands in Files, Downloads.
  2. Make a Cloudflare accountGo to dash.cloudflare.com and sign up. Confirm your email.
  3. Buy your domainIn Cloudflare tap Domain registration, then Register domains. Search your name and buy it.
  4. Upload your siteTap Workers and Pages, then Create, then the Pages tab, then Upload assets. Name it and pick your zip. Tap Deploy. You get a free yourname.pages.dev link right away.
  5. Connect your domainIn your project tap Custom domains, then Set up a custom domain. Type your domain. Because you bought it on Cloudflare, it connects itself. The lock (HTTPS) is automatic.
  6. Turn on sellingMake a Stripe account and add your bank. In Stripe go to Payment Links, then New. Add the product and price. Turn on collecting the shipping address and add shipping rates. Copy the link.
  7. Paste your buy linksGo to yourdomain.com/admin.html. Open the item and paste the link in Buy link. Download again, then upload again in Cloudflare with Create deployment.
  8. Ship itStripe emails you and the buyer on every sale. Buy the label at USPS Click-N-Ship or Pirate Ship and send it out.
