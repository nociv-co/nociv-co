Paste in a batch of research to review

For when someone's handed you a list of markets or expos to look over, whether that's from a chat with me or notes from anywhere else. One opportunity per line, format is Name, then a pipe symbol, then Location, then another pipe, then Notes, like this:
Buy Black Marketplace | Archwood at Eastlake, Phoenix | vendor sign up on their site, no cost listed yet
Location and notes are optional, a line with just a name still works. Everything comes in marked Researching so you can review and update each one with real numbers before deciding.
