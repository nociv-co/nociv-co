Pricing Cheat Sheet what you actually keep per sale, before you set a price anywhere
What this is for A scratchpad, nothing here saves anywhere. Play with a price before you ever add a product, pick which channel it'll sell through, the fee math changes depending on that, and watch what you'd actually keep. Once you land on a number you like, that's what goes in the real Add a Product form below.
The fees, plainly
  • Stripe, online checkout: 2.9% of the price, plus 30¢ flat. This is the standard card rate everywhere, Square and PayPal land in the same place, it isn't a Nociv-specific markup.
  • Stripe, Tap to Pay in person: 2.6% plus 10¢ flat. Already cheaper than online, this is your best-fee option for pop-ups.
  • Cash, Venmo, or Cash App (in person): no card fee at all if someone just hands you cash or pays a personal Venmo/Cash App, though a business account on those can carry its own small fee, check whichever one you actually use.
  • Vinted or Depop: their own separate cut, never Stripe, and it changes over time, check their current seller fee before you price something for those platforms specifically.
  • Printify or print-on-demand markup: whatever their platform fee is on top of the print cost, that's the Platform Fee % field below.

Cross-Listing & External Sales Vinted, Depop, in-person, all in one place
What this is for You don't need an automatic sync to Vinted or Depop to stay on top of things, this does the same job by hand. On any product below in the Products section, tap Vinted, Depop, or In-Person to mark where it's currently listed, those buttons turn green when active, tap again to turn them off once it's sold or pulled. Tap Copy Listing Text to copy a ready-to-paste block, name, price, description, so you're not retyping the same listing by hand on a second platform. The moment something actually sells anywhere other than your own site, Tap to Pay included, hit Mark Sold Elsewhere on that product, pick where it sold, and it logs the sale here and takes it off your tracked stock automatically, so your inventory numbers stay true no matter which platform it moved on. This is your one source of truth for what's actually sold and what you've still got, that's the whole point.
