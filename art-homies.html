THE LAND INITIATIVE

A nonprofit vision. Built in phases, starting now.

The Problem

Learning to build a mobile home, a van conversion, or an off-grid studio takes tools, land, and materials most people don't have access to, just to start learning.

The Solution — Hands-On Workshops

Community workshops teach aircrete dome construction, off-grid power, and natural building by building the site in real time. Proceeds go toward filling the void of material, land, and tool costs.

The Residency

Finished domes become a 1-month artist residency: a bed, A/C, and dedicated studio space per dome. Shared indoor/outdoor kitchen, outdoor showers, and a covered outdoor bay wide enough to build a tiny home or convert a van.

The Materials Store

An on-site hub for builders and local artists to get raw materials at cost.
