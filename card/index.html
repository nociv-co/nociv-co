PROGet them printed at Vistaprint

No printer, or want thick, clean cut cards? Vistaprint prints them cheap and mails them to you. Starts around 50 cards for $10. Printing the back costs a little extra. Prices change, check at checkout.

2. Open Vistaprint
  1. Pick your paper and corners, then tap Upload your design, then Upload from this device.
  2. Pick the Vistaprint PDF you saved. It is made at Vistaprint's exact size, so it fills their frame. Do not stretch or resize it.
  3. Made a back? Tap the back side, upload the same PDF and pick page 2.
  4. Check the preview. Words and the QR code should sit inside the dashed line.
  5. Order the smallest pack first. Scan the QR on the real card before you order a big stack.
